DC support

DC is a zero-brokerage property app for Chennai. Rent, buy or sell homes, PGs, flatmates, commercial spaces and plots, and deal directly with the owner. This page is where to reach us.

Common questions

I cannot sign in. DC signs you in with a one-time code sent to your Indian mobile number. Check the number you entered, wait for the SMS, and request a new code if it does not arrive.

How do I contact an owner? Open the listing and use Chat, or Request a visit. Some owners also allow their number to be revealed to signed-in members.

Is DC really free? Yes. There is no brokerage and no listing fee for tenants or owners.

How do I report a listing? Open the listing, choose Report, and tell us what is wrong. You can also email us.
