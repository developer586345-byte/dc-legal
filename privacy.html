What DC does

DC connects property owners and authorised representatives with people looking for homes, shared accommodation, commercial spaces and plots. This policy covers the DC mobile app and its account-support process.

Information you provide

We process your mobile number and verification credentials for account access; optional profile name and city; property descriptions, prices, amenities, photos and location pins you choose to publish; saved properties and searches; messages, visit requests, service enquiries, reports and support requests. Draft listings remain associated with your account until you publish or delete them. We also store block relationships to enforce your safety choices. Block participants can be aware of restrictions; blocking does not make public listings private.

Location and photos

Location access is requested when you choose a location feature. Device location can be used to find your city or place a map pin. A pin you confirm for a property is saved and publicly visible. You may use manual locality entry or a nearby landmark instead. We do not request background location. The photo picker provides photos you select for listing upload; the app does not require access to your whole photo library. Do not upload identity documents, private contact information or other people’s photos without permission.

Public and private content

Published listing details, property photos and selected map pins can be viewed by other users and visitors and may be copied by them. Saved searches and private drafts are restricted to your account. Messages are available to conversation participants; visit information is available to the participants. Your login phone number is never displayed publicly; it is shown only to signed-in members, and only for listings where you turn on calls (see below). Authorised support and infrastructure operators may access data as needed to operate, support or protect the service. Messages are not end-to-end encrypted.

Owner calls, alerts and listing statistics

When an owner turns on “Let signed-in seekers call me” for a listing, a signed-in member who taps “View phone number” sees the owner’s registered mobile number and first name. Each reveal is recorded, the owner is notified, and a member can reveal at most 25 numbers in 24 hours. Owners can switch any listing to chat-only at any time. DC does not share a seeker’s phone number with the owner. Owners see totals of views by signed-in members, shortlists, chats, visit requests and number reveals for their own listings. If you save a search, we compare new listings against it and notify you in the app. Recently viewed listings and your preferred city are stored on your device. If you allow notifications, we store a device token for your account and send chat, visit and alert notifications through Google Firebase Cloud Messaging; the token is removed when you sign out. “Use my current location” reads your location on your phone only to find your Chennai area; DC does not store your GPS position.

How information is used

We use information to authenticate accounts, display and search listings, enable messages and visit requests, save your preferences, handle enquiries and complaints, prevent misuse and maintain the service. DC currently does not process rental payments or purchases and does not include advertising or advertising-ID tracking.

Service providers and external apps

Supabase provides authentication, database, image storage and realtime communication. Fast2SMS and telecom providers process phone numbers and verification messages for OTP delivery. Google Maps, when configured, processes map requests and may collect device/network information under Google’s policies. Device geocoding services process address/location searches. These services can receive IP addresses, request metadata and diagnostic logs. Data may be processed outside your country according to provider infrastructure and contracts. External map, sharing, phone and email apps operate under their own privacy policies. We do not sell your personal information.

Security

Network requests use HTTPS where supported by the integrated services. Supabase access rules restrict account data and conversations. Access controls cannot prevent recipients from copying content you share, and no system is completely secure. Never share an OTP or send payment based only on a listing.

Retention and deletion

Account data is retained while needed to provide the service, maintain your listings and handle requests. You can edit listings, remove saved items and delete drafts. To delete your account and associated data, use Account → Delete account or email theeasterncult@gmail.com with the subject “DC account deletion” and your registered mobile number. You do not need to reinstall the app. We may verify control of that number before processing; never send an OTP, password or identity document by email. Support processes requests manually and will tell you the expected completion date. Deletion covers your profile, listings, stored property photos, drafts, saved items, messages and linked requests in our active systems. Limited records may be retained where necessary for an unresolved complaint, fraud prevention or a legal obligation; we will explain the category and reason when applicable. Provider backups and operational logs expire under provider retention schedules and may not be immediately erased. Copies already shared or independently saved by other people are outside our direct control.

Your choices and questions

You can browse without signing in, deny optional device permissions, use manual location entry, edit your information and request correction or deletion through support. Contact theeasterncult@gmail.com for privacy questions or complaints. We may update this policy as features or data practices change and will update the effective date.

Age suitability

DC is intended for adults aged 18 and over. It is not directed at children. If you believe a child has provided personal data, contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action.
