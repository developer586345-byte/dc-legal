Delete your DC account

You can request account deletion without reinstalling DC.

Send a request

Email theeasterncult@gmail.com with subject “DC account deletion”. Include the mobile number registered with DC. Alternatively open DC → Account → Delete account to submit a request. Support phone numbers: +91 7305240210 and +91 8754020131.

What happens next

Support will verify that you control the account, confirm the scope of the request and tell you the expected completion date. Never send an OTP or password in an email. Requests are processed manually; this is a deletion request, not an instant erase button.

Delete some data without deleting your account

You do not have to close your account to remove your content. In the app you can delete any listing or draft from My listings, remove photos from a listing while editing it, clear saved homes from Shortlist, and remove a saved search from its alert. To have other data removed — for example a conversation, a visit request or a service enquiry — email theeasterncult@gmail.com with the subject “DC delete my data”, the mobile number registered with DC, and what you want removed. Support will confirm the scope and tell you the expected completion date. Your account stays active.

Data covered

Your account/profile, listings and property photos, drafts, saved homes/searches, messages and linked visit/enquiry/support records in active systems are included. Limited records required for unresolved complaints, fraud prevention or legal obligations may be retained, with the category and reason explained to you. Provider logs/backups expire under their own retention schedules. Copies saved independently by other users are outside our control. See the Privacy Policy for more information.
